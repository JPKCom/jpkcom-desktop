# About JPKCom Desktop

A desktop-style web interface — windows, a menu bar, a Dock and apps, right in the browser.
Free software under the MIT License.

## What it is

JPKCom Desktop turns a website into a small desktop. Content pages open in windows, bookmarks
and examples live in collections, and a handful of apps — editor, notes, tasks, calculator,
terminal, media player and a fortune cookie — keep their data right in your browser.

- **No build step, no dependencies at runtime.** Native ES modules, served as they are.
- **A strict Content Security Policy.** No inline scripts or styles, no `innerHTML`, no `eval`.
- **Any number of languages.** Every text comes from a locale file.
- **Accessible.** Keyboard everywhere, names for screen readers, reduced motion respected.
- **Private by default.** Online services ask before their first request.
