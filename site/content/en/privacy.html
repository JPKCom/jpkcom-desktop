What stays in your browser

The desktop sets no cookies and uses no tracking. Your preferences (language, mode, wallpaper, Dock) and the content of the apps (notes, tasks, editor documents, calculator history) are stored in your browser's local storage — on your device only. They never reach the server. Settings → Reset deletes them; Settings → Data → Backup saves them as a file.
