About JPKCom Desktop

A desktop-style web interface — windows, a menu bar, a Dock and apps, right in the browser. Free software under the MIT License.

What it is

JPKCom Desktop turns a website into a small desktop. Content pages open in windows, bookmarks and examples live in collections, and a handful of apps — editor, notes, tasks, calculator, terminal, media player and a fortune cookie — keep their data right in your browser.

  • No build step, no dependencies at runtime. Native ES modules, served as they are.
  • A strict Content Security Policy. No inline scripts or styles, no innerHTML, no eval.
  • Any number of languages. Every text comes from a locale file; plurals, dates and numbers follow the language.
  • Accessible. Every part works with the keyboard, controls have names for screen readers, and reduced motion is respected.
  • Private by default. Online services are off until the site offers them — and then each one asks before its first request.
