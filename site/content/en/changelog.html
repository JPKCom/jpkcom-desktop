Changelog

What changed in which version of JPKCom Desktop.

1.2.0

Fresh data, your own icons and more ways to link a site into the desktop.

  • Feeds, sayings and everything under site/data/ and site/content/ are fetched fresh again, even with the fast start from the offline copy — a new feed item no longer offers a new version.
  • Site icon sets: your own icons next to the built-in ones, including two-tone icons (iconSets).
  • Manual pages for the terminal's man per entry, collection and language — try man writing-pages.
  • Web windows remember their page anywhere on the site and can share links to a sub-page; a collection can open its web overview in a window of its own.
  • The Fortune app can be renamed, run online only and take its online sources from your own modules.
  • A site that used another service worker before can remove its old caches (offline.legacyCaches).

1.1.0

A faster start and more for site owners.

  • Windows load their code when they first open: the desktop starts with far fewer files.
  • The start asks for all its files at once, and a repeat visit starts from the offline copy — a new version is offered with a reload.
  • A house theme for your site (site/theme.css): corners, glass and shadows through documented tokens.
  • Your own apps can keep their texts next to their code; the example app “Hello” shows how.
  • A live demo, a template repository and a quickstart in English and German.

1.0.0

The first public release.

  • Window manager with snapping, a tile menu on the zoom button, the window overview and session restore.
  • Menu bar, Dock, desktop icons, “All apps”, context menus, keyboard shortcuts and notification banners.
  • Settings, wallpapers (generated motifs, gradients, colours, pictures), backup and restore, trash.
  • Reader for content pages, image viewer, Catalog for collections, search, calendar with public holidays, weather and news from a JSON Feed.
  • Apps: editor, notes, tasks, calculator, terminal, audio and video player, and the fortune cookie.
  • Any number of languages, a strict Content Security Policy and no build step.
