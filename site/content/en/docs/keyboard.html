Keyboard

Everything works without a mouse. Ctrl stands for ⌘ on keyboards that have it.

Everywhere

KeysAction
Ctrl+KOpen or close the search
F3 or Ctrl+↑Show all windows side by side; arrow keys and Enter pick one, Esc goes back
Ctrl+`Next window (the key to the left of 1)
Ctrl+Shift+`Previous window
Shift+F10Context menu of the focused element
Tab, Shift+TabMove the focus forward or back

Menus and Dock

KeysAction
← →Move between the menus of the menu bar
↓, Enter, SpaceOpen a menu; arrow keys move through its entries
EscClose the menu, the focus returns
Alt+← →Move the focused app in the Dock

In the apps

AppKeys
EditorCtrl+S save, Ctrl+O open, Ctrl+F find, Alt+T new tab, Alt+W close tab, Alt+Page Up Page Down previous or next tab, Alt+Z word wrap
Image ViewerCtrl+O open, Ctrl+I info
Audio and Video PlayerSpace or K play or pause, ← → seek, M mute, N P next or previous, F full screen (video), Ctrl+O open, Ctrl+I info
CatalogArrow keys move, Enter or a double click opens
Terminal↑ ↓ history, Tab completes, Ctrl+C cancels, Ctrl+L clears, Ctrl+U clears the line
Notes↑ ↓ walk the list, Ctrl+F search
TasksEnter edits, Alt+↑ ↓ moves a task
CalculatorDigits and + - * /, Enter calculates, Esc clears, Ctrl+C copies the result
FortuneSpace or N next, ← previous, Ctrl+C copies

The window How it works in the Help menu lists the shortcuts of everything that is loaded right now.
